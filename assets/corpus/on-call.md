# On-call

On-call rotates weekly, handing over on Wednesday at 10:00 Europe/Lisbon. The handover
is a live conversation, not a message - the outgoing engineer walks the incoming one
through anything still open.

## Expectations

Acknowledge a page within 15 minutes. If you cannot, escalate to the secondary rather
than letting it time out. Nobody is expected to fix everything alone; the on-call
engineer's job is to make sure something is happening, not to be the one doing it.

You are not expected to work your normal hours during a heavy on-call week. Take the
time back.

## Compensation

On-call weeks are compensated at a flat weekly rate, plus an hourly rate for time spent
responding outside working hours. Submit hours in the same ticket as the incident.

## What does not page

Cost alerts, non-urgent capacity warnings, and anything on a staging environment. If
you are being paged by staging, that alert is misconfigured - fix the alert.

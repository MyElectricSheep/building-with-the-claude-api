# Expenses and travel

Spend company money as if it were your own, but do not be silly about it. The policy
below exists so nobody has to guess.

## Limits without pre-approval

- Meals while travelling: 60 EUR per day
- Books and courses: 500 EUR per year
- Software and hardware under 200 EUR
- Coworking day passes

Anything above these needs manager approval **before** you spend it. Approval after the
fact is at your manager's discretion and is not guaranteed.

## Travel

Book economy on flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed above that. Trains
are preferred to flights where the journey is under five hours - it is usually faster
door to door and it is company policy for carbon reasons.

## Receipts

Submit within 30 days with a photo of the receipt. Expenses submitted after 60 days are
not reimbursed except in genuinely exceptional circumstances.
